---
title: Resident API
description: A resident's own leases, payments, requests and profile.
---

What a signed-in resident sees and does: their leases and balances, payments and AutoPay, maintenance and service requests,
renewals, notifications and profile.

Calls carry the **access token** of a signed-in customer or resident as a bearer token. See
[Customer sign-in with PKCE](/docs/customer-sign-in).

With the SDK: `ResidentClient`, and `@unitfield/react` for React.
